# Own Orbit

Own Orbit is an offline-first planner for iPhone and iPad. It keeps your tasks
in lists with dates, repeats and reminders, shows them next to the events from
your device's calendars, tracks your habits and streaks, and keeps a Shelf of
the movies, TV series and books you're into.

Your records stay on your device by default. Unlimited lists, habits and Shelf
items, subscribed calendars, iCloud Sync and every widget are available with
Own Orbit Pro.
