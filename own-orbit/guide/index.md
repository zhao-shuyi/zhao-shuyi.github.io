# Own Orbit User Guide

Own Orbit is an offline-first planner for iPhone and iPad. It keeps your tasks in lists with dates, repeats and reminders, shows them next to the events from your device's calendars, tracks your habits and streaks, and keeps a Shelf of the movies, TV series and books you're into. Your records are stored on your device by default.

## Getting Started

Own Orbit starts almost empty: an **Inbox** list for your tasks and three built-in Shelf types (**Movies**, **TV series** and **Books**). There is no sign-up and no sample data.

The five tabs are:

- **Today** — the day at a glance: events, tasks, habits and what you're watching or reading.
- **Calendar** — Day, 3 Days, Week, Month and Year views, plus the **List** view with all your lists and tasks.
- **Habit** — your habits, a week strip, and check-ins.
- **Shelf** — your movies, TV series and books.
- **Settings** — General, Calendars, Habits, Shelf, iCloud Sync and About.

On iPad, and on wide windows, each tab splits into two panes: pick something on the left and its details open on the right.

Own Orbit asks for calendar access the first time you open a calendar view, and for notifications the first time you set a reminder — never at launch.

## Today

Under the date, up to four cards: **Events**, **Tasks**, **Habits** and **Shelf**. Tap a card's chevron to fold it. Cards with nothing in them start folded and open by themselves when something arrives. Everything updates as the day goes on, after midnight, and when you change time zone.

- **Events** lists today's events from your calendars, all-day ones first. Turn the card off with **Settings > Calendars > Events in Today**.
- **Tasks** shows overdue tasks first (in coral), then timed tasks in time order, then all-day tasks. Tap the circle to complete a task.
- **Habits** lists the habits due today by part of the day. Tap the check to check in.
- **Shelf** lists everything in progress, with **+1** to log the next episode or page.

Tap the floating **+** to add a task due today.

## Tasks and Lists

Open **Calendar** and choose **List** from the view menu. You'll see a search field, your device and subscribed calendars, **All tasks**, the **Inbox**, then your folders and lists with their counts of open tasks.

- **Add a task** with the floating **+**. Type a title and press **Return** to save; the field clears for the next one. The controls under the title set the **Date**, **Priority** (tap to cycle), a **Tag**, and the **List**. The arrow at the right saves the task and opens it in full.
- **Complete** a task by tapping its circle; tap again to undo. **Swipe left** for Delete and Skip, or **press and hold** for Open, Complete, Skip and Delete. Deleting always asks first.
- **Skip** a task you've decided not to do: it leaves your open lists without counting as done.
- **Task Detail** (tap a task) saves every change at once: date, reminders, list, priority, tags, **Subtasks** and **Notes**. A task with some subtasks ticked shows as in progress.
- **Tags** are plain text: "Errands" and "errands" are the same tag.
- A list groups its tasks into **Overdue**, **Today**, **Upcoming** and **No date**. Its last row, **Completed & Skipped**, holds its history; tap a task's symbol there to restore it.
- Use the **⋯** menu of the List view for **New List**, **New Folder** and **Edit Lists**. A list has an emoji, a name, a colour and an optional folder. Deleting a list moves its tasks to the Inbox; deleting a folder keeps its lists. The Inbox can't be deleted.
- A list's **⋯** menu has **Sort**: by due date, priority, title or date created.

### Dates and times

**Date & Time** has shortcuts (Today, Tomorrow, Next week, No date), a calendar, and **Starts**, **Ends**, an **All-day** switch and **Time zone**. A timed task keeps the time zone it was set in, so a 9:00 London task shows at the matching time when you're in Tokyo. A task is overdue once its end has passed.

### Repeating tasks

Tap **Repeat** (a task needs a date first): Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly or **Custom** — every few days, chosen weekdays, chosen days of the month, or "the last Friday", ending never, on a date, or after a number of times. Completing or skipping a repeating task affects only that date, and the next one appears straight away. If you fall behind, only the latest missed occurrence stays overdue; earlier ones are skipped automatically and kept in the history. Changing the rule applies from today and never rewrites the past.

### Reminders

A task can have several reminders: at the due time, a few minutes, an hour or a day before, or a custom time. All-day tasks remind you at the **Default reminder time** (**Settings > General**, 09:00 at first). Tap a reminder to open its task. If notifications are off, the reminder list shows a notice with a button to open iOS Settings.

The **App icon badge** (**Settings > General**) can count today's tasks, today's plus overdue tasks, or nothing.

## Search

Tap the search field at the top of the List view, or **Search** in a list's or calendar view's **⋯** menu. Search finds tasks by title, notes and tags, and events by title, location and notes, ignoring case and accents. Narrow it with the **All / Tasks / Events** chips and the **Priority**, **Status** and **Tags** pills. Start with **#** to search by tag. Recent searches are kept on this device; swipe one away or tap **Clear**.

## Calendar

Choose a view from the menu at the top left (on iPad, the segmented control): **Day**, **3 Days**, **Week**, **Month**, **Year** or **List**. Swipe sideways to move, tap the title to jump to any date, and tap **Today** to come back.

- Events show with their calendar's colour; your tasks show in their list's colour.
- **Tap an empty slot** to add a task at that time, or **press and hold an empty slot and drag down** to draw a task of the length you want.
- **Press and hold a task** to lift it, then drag it to another time or day. For a repeating task, only that occurrence moves. Drag the bottom edge of a timed task to change its length.
- **Tap a day** (in Month, or a day's header) to see its agenda, with **Add task** at the end.
- **Tap an event** for its details: time, calendar, location, link and notes, plus **Open in Calendar**. Own Orbit only reads your events; it never changes them.

**Settings > Calendars** shows your calendar access, lets you switch individual calendars on and off for this device, and sets the **Default view**, whether completed tasks show on the calendar, and **Events in Today**.

### Subscribed calendars (Pro)

Add a calendar published on the web — a sports season, a school term, a team schedule — under **Settings > Calendars > Add**: paste its address (webcal or https), give it a name and pick a colour. Own Orbit checks the address before saving. Its events show read-only alongside your others and refresh when the app opens (at most every 30 minutes), in the background, and when you tap **Refresh** in its editor. The last good copy stays available offline. After three failed refreshes in a row, a warning appears next to its name.

## Habits

Open the **Habit** tab and tap **+**. Give the habit a name, an emoji and a colour, then choose **How often** (every day, chosen weekdays, or a number of times a week), the **Part of the day**, and an optional **Reminder**.

- **Check in** by tapping a habit's check; tap again to undo. **Press and hold** for Done, Skip, Clear and Open. A skipped day never counts against you.
- **The week strip** at the top shows each day's progress. Swipe it back to earlier weeks, and tap a day to check in or fix a past day. Future days can't be changed.
- **"Times a week" habits** show their progress in the row ("2/3 this week") and don't count toward the day's total, so every day can still reach 100%.
- **Habit Detail** shows your **current** and **best streak**, your **last 30 days**, and a monthly history you can tap to fix a day.
- **Overview** shows every habit by week or month.
- Use the **⋯** menu to **Reorder Habits** or see **Archived Habits**. Archiving hides a habit but keeps its history; deleting it removes the history too.

**Settings > Habits** sets the default times for Morning, Afternoon and Evening and whether completed habits stay in place, move to the bottom, or are hidden.

## Shelf

Open the **Shelf** tab and tap **+** to add a movie, a TV series or a book.

- Every item has a **status**: **Want to**, **In progress**, **Done** or **Dropped**.
- **Progress** counts episodes, pages, or your own unit. Tap **+1** on the list (or on Today) to log one more; tap the numbers to set any value. Starting progress moves an item to In progress, and reaching the total marks it Done — and moving it back undoes that.
- Start and finish dates fill in by themselves and can be edited. When something reaches Done, Own Orbit offers to **score** it from 1 to 10.
- **Covers.** Each item gets a drawn cover in its type's emoji and colour. Tap the poster to change them, or **Add photo** to use a picture from your library (only the photo you pick is shared with Own Orbit), then drag and pinch to frame it.
- Switch between the **Shelf** grid and the **List** with the menu at the top left. The **filter bar** chooses a type and a status, and **Sort** orders by date added, recently updated, title, score or date finished.
- **Overview** shows a year at a glance: what you finished, your average score, and items finished by month.

**Settings > Shelf** lets you change each type's emoji, unit and colour, hide a type (its items are kept), add your own types (Pro), and choose whether new items start as Want to or In progress.

## Widgets

Add Own Orbit widgets from the iOS widget gallery.

- **Today** (free; small, medium, large) — today's tasks and events, with a **+** to add a task. Tap a ring to complete a task.
- **Habits** (Pro) — tap to check in.
- **Shelf** (Pro) — what you're in the middle of, with **+1**.
- **Calendar**, **Week overview** and **Month overview** (Pro) — your events and tasks by day, week or month.
- **Lock Screen** (Pro) — your next event and task, or a count you choose.

Widgets show your data on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, so anyone who can see your screen can see them.

## Free and Own Orbit Pro

Own Orbit is free to use. Without Pro you can have **five lists** (the Inbox counts as one), **three active habits** and **three Shelf items per type**, plus the Today widget. Nothing is ever deleted or locked because of these limits.

**Own Orbit Pro** is a one-time, non-consumable purchase — not a subscription — that removes those limits and unlocks your own Shelf types, subscribed calendars, **iCloud Sync** and every widget. You'll see it when you try to go past a free limit or tap a Pro feature.

**Restore Pro:** if you purchased Pro before with the same Apple ID, open the Own Orbit Pro screen (for example, **Settings > iCloud Sync**, then tap the locked **Enable Sync** row) and tap **Restore Purchases**. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple.

## Turn Off Habits or Shelf

Don't need habits or the Shelf? Switch them off under **Settings > General > Features**. Their tab, their Today card, their reminders and their widget go away; nothing is deleted, and everything returns exactly as it was when you switch them back on.

## iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is an optional Pro feature that syncs your Own Orbit data across your own devices through your private iCloud database. It is off by default. Calendar events and the downloaded copies of subscribed calendars are never synced.

Go to **Settings > iCloud Sync** and turn on **Enable Sync**. When prompted:

- Choose **Keep This Device Data** to keep this device's data and merge it with what iCloud already has.
- Choose **Restore Existing iCloud Data** after a reinstall or on a new device, when iCloud already has the data you want. This **replaces** the data on this device with what's in iCloud, after a safety copy is saved on the device first. It can't be undone.

Own Orbit then asks to close, and applies the change when you reopen it. After a restore you may see **Waiting for iCloud data** while your records arrive; if nothing arrives, a **Use Defaults Instead** button appears.

The **Status** card shows your iCloud account, the sync status (**Waiting for restart**, **Waiting for iCloud**, **Syncing…**, **Idle**, or **Error**), and the last sync time. Keep Own Orbit open while an important sync is in progress, and allow other devices some time to receive changes. Avoid editing the same item on two devices at once. If a sync leaves duplicates behind, Own Orbit tidies them up and tells you once with **Sync adjusted N items**.

To turn sync off, flip the toggle; it applies after the next restart. If a sync is still finishing, Own Orbit asks you to try again in a moment so no change is left behind. Your data stays on the device and in iCloud. If your Pro purchase is no longer active while sync is on, you'll see **Sync is paused** — your data is safe on the device, but changes no longer reach your other devices.

## Delete Data

Delete individual tasks, lists, folders, habits, Shelf items and subscribed calendars from their own screens. Own Orbit asks before deleting. Deleting a list moves its tasks to the Inbox, and deleting a folder keeps its lists. A custom Shelf type can be deleted only when it is empty.

Own Orbit has no "delete everything" button. To remove your Own Orbit data from iCloud without touching your device, turn sync off, then use **Delete iCloud Data** in **Settings > iCloud Sync** (it needs Pro). This can't be undone, and other devices will lose access to the iCloud copy.

Deleting the app removes its data from that device. It does not remove data already stored in your iCloud account.
