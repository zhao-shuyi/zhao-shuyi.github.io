# Pack Log

Pack Log is an offline-first travel packing app for iPhone and iPad. It keeps an
inventory of what you own, helps you build packing lists from reusable kits,
tracks what is out at each hotel so you never leave anything behind, and guides
you through putting everything back at home.

Your records stay on your device by default. Optional iCloud Sync and unlimited
trips and kits are available with Pack Log Pro.
