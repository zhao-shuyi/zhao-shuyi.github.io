## 4. iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is optional and requires Pack Log Pro, an Apple ID, iCloud availability, and the app's sync setting to be turned on.

When iCloud Sync is on, your Pack Log data may sync across devices connected to the same Apple ID. Changes made on one device may appear on other synced devices. If the same record is edited on two devices around the same time, one version wins in its entirety; Pack Log checks for and repairs certain conflicts after syncing (for example, two devices both marking a different trip as active) and tells you when it has adjusted something. Deleting or editing data while sync is on may affect other synced devices.

Enabling sync lets you either keep this device's data and merge it with iCloud, or replace this device's data with what is in iCloud ("Restore Existing iCloud Data"). Review the prompt carefully before choosing; replacing cannot be undone. Turning sync on or off takes effect after the app restarts. To limit risk, "Delete iCloud Data" is only available while iCloud Sync is turned off, and Pack Log does not offer a "delete everything on this device" action, so a single action cannot unexpectedly empty your device, your iCloud account, and your other devices at once.

Apple operates iCloud and CloudKit. Your use of iCloud is governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.
