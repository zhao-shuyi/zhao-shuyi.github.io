# Pack Log User Guide

Pack Log is an offline-first travel packing app for iPhone and iPad. It keeps an inventory of what you own, helps you build packing lists from reusable kits, tracks what is packed and what is out during a trip, helps you sweep a hotel room before checkout, and guides you through putting everything back at home. Your records are stored on your device by default.

## Getting Started

Pack Log starts with sample kits, starter categories and storage locations, plus two short starter lists: a **home checklist** for leaving your house ("Lock all doors and windows") and a **"Before you close the door"** list of hotel hiding spots to check at checkout ("Safe", "Power sockets"). Use them as they are or change them any time.

The four tabs are:

- **Trip** — the active trip's packing list, in three modes: **Packing**, **Traveling**, and **Unpacking**.
- **Logbook** — all your trips: create, activate, review, and delete them.
- **Inventory** — everything you own, and where each thing is right now.
- **Settings** — kits, categories, storage locations, your home checklist, iCloud Sync, and About.

A typical journey is: build your inventory and kits → create a trip in the Logbook → pack → travel (sweep each hotel room) → unpack → complete the trip.

## Build Your Inventory

Open the **Inventory** tab and tap **+** to add an item. Give it a name; the photo, category, and home location are optional.

- **Photo:** tap **Add Photo**, then **Take Photo** or **Choose from Library**. Pack Log asks for camera access only the first time you take a photo, and the photo picker shares only the photos you select.
- **Category:** group your things (Clothes, Skincare, Digital…). Manage them under **Settings > Category Management**.
- **Home location:** where the item lives at home ("Wardrobe shelf 2"). Pick one or create one on the spot; manage them under **Settings > Storage Locations**.

Items without a category appear under **Uncategorized** and without a location under **Unsorted** — you can fill those in later. You can also add items while you're packing (see "Link an entry to your inventory").

The line under each item's name shows where it is right now: its home location, or an emphasized status such as **✓ Packed · Kyoto · Carry-on**, **↗ In use · Bathroom**, or **○ Planned · Kyoto**. Use the **Where is it?** search to find anything.

## Kits

A kit is a reusable bundle of entries — "Beach", "Ski", or your own "Camera bag" — organized in sections. Pack Log comes with eight sample kits.

Go to **Settings > Kit Management** to edit, delete, reorder, or create kits. A kit needs a name, an emoji, and at least one entry. Type a name in a section's **Add entry** row; use the stepper beside it to add several at once ("T-shirt ×5" creates five entries). Tap an entry's emoji to change it, tap its name to rename it, and tap **›** to link it to an inventory item.

When a trip's list turns out just right, open the completed trip in the Logbook and tap **Save List as Kit** — see "Complete the trip."

## Plan a Trip

Go to the **Logbook** tab and tap **+**.

- **Name and emoji** — both required (the emoji defaults to 🧳).
- **Dates** — a start date and, by default, an end date a week later. Switch **End Date** off for an open-ended trip.
- **Room zones** — places in a hotel room (Bedroom, Bathroom, Safe…). Used when you mark an item "in use."
- **Bags** — Carry-on, Checked Luggage, Day bag… Used to say where an item is packed. Both lists are pre-filled and are optional; the first trip gets a worked example and later trips copy your last trip's lists.
- **Copy kits in** — tick the kits you want. Tap a kit to preview its sections and entries. Each kit's sections arrive as separate sections of your trip.

Tap **Create Trip**. A new trip starts as **PLANNED**; tap its **PLANNED** pill to make it the **active** trip shown on the Trip tab. (If another trip is already active, you'll be asked to confirm — its progress is kept, and you can switch back any time.) Tap the active trip's **ACTIVE** pill to send it back to planned.

Tap a trip in the Logbook (or the gear button on the Trip tab) to open **Edit Trip**: change the details, add, rename, reorder, or delete bags and room zones (tap **Edit** on a section), copy more kits in, or delete the trip. Long-press a trip row for **Activate Trip**, **Move to Planned**, or **Delete Trip**.

## Pack

On the **Trip** tab, **Packing** mode shows your list in sections.

- **Check off** an item with the circle at the left. Packed items count toward the section's "5/7 packed" progress bar.
- **Tap a row** to open its details.
- **Add an entry** with the **Add entry** row at the bottom of a section; use the stepper to add several at once. Add sections with **+ Add Section** below the list.
- **Section | Bag** switch at the top: view the list by section, or by bag (items with no bag appear under **No bag**).
- **Assign a bag** by tapping the small bag chip on a row (or **＋ Bag** if it has none) and choosing one. The chip shows once your trip has bags; to create or rename bags, use Edit Trip. In bag view, rows show their section instead.
- **Collapse** a card with the chevron to hide its rows — handy once a section is done.

Tap **⋯** on a card for bulk actions: **Pack All**, **Unpack All**, **Mark All In Use**, **Re-pack All** and, in section view, **Edit Entries**, **Rename Section**, **Move Up/Down**, **Delete Section**, and **Assign All to Bag**. In bag view **⋯** also lets you move the bag up or down. Actions that change several items at once show an **Undo** at the bottom of the screen.

**Edit Entries** puts a section into edit mode: delete entries, change quantities with the stepper, and move rows up or down. Tap **Done** when finished.

### Quantities

"T-shirt ×5" is five separate entries shown as one tidy row. Tap the row to expand it and see each one: you can pack, bag, mark dirty, or link just one. The checkbox on the grouped row packs the rest of the group (or, when everything is packed, unpacks it). To change how many you have, use **⋯ > Edit Entries** and the stepper. Rename one entry and it separates from the group.

### Link an entry to your inventory

Tap an entry, then **Link to Inventory**, and pick an item (or **Create New Inventory Item**). A linked entry keeps its own name but shows the item's photo and its home location, which Unpacking uses. An inventory item can be linked to only one entry across your open trips — others show as **Unavailable**. When you copy a kit whose item is already taken, the entry arrives unlinked with a **Link unavailable** note.

### Home checklist

At the bottom of the section view, the **Home Checklist** has tasks like "Lock all doors and windows." Tick them off, add your own with **Add task**, or tap **⋯ > Edit Tasks** to rename, reorder, or delete them for this trip. Each new trip starts with a copy of your master list, which you edit under **Settings > Home Checklist**.

### Start the trip

**Start Trip** becomes available once everything is packed and the home checklist is complete, and switches the trip to **Traveling**. You can also switch modes any time with the control at the top.

## Travel

In **Traveling** mode your list is grouped into **To Pack** (only if something is left), **In Use**, and **Packed**.

- **Mark something in use** by tapping its circle. If your trip has room zones, a bar asks where it went — tap a zone, or **Skip**. Tap an in-use item again to put it back.
- **Bag / Room** switch: group stowed items by bag and in-use items by room zone.
- **Dirty clothes:** tap the 🧺 beside an item to flag it dirty (tap again for clean). On a grouped row, 🧺 flips the whole group. Dirty items show a **Dirty** badge.
- **Bulk Edit** (on each group's header, or in a card's **⋯**) lets you select several items and act on them together: put back, set a room zone, mark in use, or change the bag.
- Change an item's bag or zone with the small chip on its row.

## Checkout Sweep

Before you leave a hotel, tap **Checkout Sweep** at the top of Traveling mode. You'll see only what is still out, grouped by room zone.

- Tap an item to put it back in its bag — it is crossed out rather than disappearing, and you can tap it again to take it back out. The chip beside it shows (and lets you change) which bag it goes into.
- Use a zone's **⋯** to **Pack All Back**, **Take All Back Out**, or **Assign All to Bag**.
- Below, **Before you close the door** lists classic hiding places — safe, sockets, charging cables, under the pillow. Tap each as you check it, and tap the pencil to edit the list for all your trips.
- If nothing is out, you'll see **Room Clear ✓** — the forget-spot list is still worth a look.

Tap **Done** to leave. You can run the sweep again at every hotel.

## Find Things

- **Where is it?** (search bar on the Inventory tab) finds your inventory items and tells you where each is right now. If there's no match you can add it to your inventory.
- On the Trip tab the same bar searches *this trip's* items only.
- The **filter** button beside the Trip search lets you show only certain states (To Pack, Packed, In Use, Dirty), bags, room zones, or link status (Linked, Unlinked, Broken Link). The filter applies in all three modes; tap **Clear All** or a chip's **×** to remove it. A filter that is still on can hide things — look for the count badge on the button.

## Unpack and Complete the Trip

In **Unpacking** mode, items are grouped by where they live at home — one band per storage location, with **Unsorted** (linked items with no home location yet) and **Unlinked** (entries not linked to your inventory) at the end. Each band holds cards by section or bag (choose with the **Section | Bag** switch). Tap an item's circle when you've put it back; tap a group's circle to put away the whole group. Use a card's **⋯** for **Put All Back** or **Mark All Still Out**. Give an item a home location in the Inventory tab if you want it to land somewhere specific next time.

When you're done, tap **Complete Trip**. If anything is unresolved you'll be shown what's left. The trip moves to the **Completed** list in the Logbook as a read-only archive — and an **Undo** appears for a few seconds in case you tapped too soon. Once it disappears, completion is final.

Open a completed trip to review exactly what happened. Tap **Save List as Kit** to turn it into a new kit (without any trip state) for next time. Delete it from here or from the Logbook if you no longer need it.

## Free and Pack Log Pro

Pack Log is free to use. Without Pro you can have **one open trip at a time** (completing a trip frees the slot) and **eight kits**, and the Logbook shows your three most recent completed trips (older ones are kept, and return with Pro). Nothing is ever deleted because of these limits.

**Pack Log Pro** is a one-time, non-consumable purchase — not a subscription — that removes those limits and unlocks **iCloud Sync**. You'll see it when you tap the locked **Enable Sync** row (**Settings > iCloud Sync**) or try to go past a free limit.

**Restore Pro:** if you purchased Pro before with the same Apple ID, open the Pack Log Pro screen (for example, **Settings > iCloud Sync**, then tap the locked **Enable Sync** row) and tap **Restore Purchases**. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple.

## iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is an optional Pro feature that syncs your Pack Log data across your own devices through your private iCloud database. It is off by default.

Go to **Settings > iCloud Sync** and turn on **Enable Sync**. When prompted:

- Choose **Keep This Device Data** to keep this device's data and merge it with what iCloud already has.
- Choose **Restore Existing iCloud Data** after a reinstall or on a new device, when iCloud already has the setup you want. This **replaces** the data on this device (including its starter data) with what's in iCloud, after a safety copy is saved on the device first. It can't be undone.

Pack Log then asks to close, and applies the change when you reopen it. After a restore you may see **Waiting for iCloud Data** for a moment while your records arrive; if your iCloud account turns out to be empty, a **Use Starter Kits Instead** button appears.

The **Status** card shows your iCloud account, the sync status (**Waiting for restart**, **Waiting for iCloud**, **Syncing…**, **Idle**, or **Error**), and the last sync time. Keep Pack Log open while an important sync is in progress, and allow other devices some time to receive changes. Avoid editing the same item on two devices at once. If a sync leaves two devices disagreeing about something (like two active trips), Pack Log tidies it up and briefly shows **Sync adjusted N items**.

To turn sync off, flip the toggle. If a sync is still finishing, Pack Log asks you to try again in a moment so no change is left behind. Your data stays on the device and in iCloud; syncing simply stops after the restart. If your Pro purchase is no longer active while sync is on, you'll see **Sync Is Paused** — your data is safe on the device, but changes no longer reach your other devices.

## Delete Data

Delete individual items, kits, trips, sections, and entries from their own screens. Pack Log asks before deleting anything that contains other things and tells you how many items are affected. Deleting a category or storage location never deletes your items.

Pack Log has no "delete everything" button. To remove your Pack Log data from iCloud without touching your device, turn sync off, then use **Delete iCloud Data** at the bottom of **Settings > iCloud Sync** (it needs Pro). This can't be undone, and other devices will lose access to the iCloud copy.

Deleting the app removes its data from that device. It does not remove data already stored in your iCloud account.
